About PawMart

A family shop on Oak Street, run by people who got into this the usual way: one rescue dog, then another, then a shop.

The Okafor family, since 2016

PawMart started when our founder, Adaeze Okafor, couldn't find honest advice — or the right food — for a rescue dog with a sensitive stomach anywhere in Springfield. Ten years on, the shop is still family-run: Adaeze and her son Chidi behind the counter, Dana on grooming, and a shop cat named Pepper who supervises from the window perch (which is, yes, for sale).

We stock what we'd feed our own: food with named ingredients, toys we'd let our dogs keep, and prices we'd pay ourselves. If we wouldn't give it to Pepper, it doesn't go on the shelf.

Do you deliver?

Yes — local delivery Mon–Sat. Free over $40, otherwise a flat $4.99. Order by 4pm and it leaves the shop the same day.

What if my pet won't eat it?

Bring the bag back within 30 days, even opened, and we'll swap it for something they'll actually eat. No questions, no fuss.

How do I book grooming or boarding?

Call us on (555) 214-0194 or ask at the counter. Boarding dogs need an up-to-date vaccination card.

Can you order something you don't stock?

Usually, yes — most brands reach us in 3–5 days. Ask at the counter and we'll call you when it lands.
